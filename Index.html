Spin a season, draft a team, then chase the championships.

    Choose a mode

    Solo

    Classic season

    Spin a year, draft a team of seven, then race one season and try to win both world championships.

    2026 season

    The current season: the real 23-race calendar and a draft pool of only the 11 teams on the 2026 grid, Cadillac and Audi included.

    Career

    Run one team through 3, 5 or 8 seasons. Start as a brand-new team, a midfield team, a top team or at random. Drivers age and retire, staff move on, and every offseason you redraft.

    Two players

    Two-player draft

    Two players, one screen, or you against the AI. You both pick from the same draws, so every pick can cost the other player. Then both teams race one season.

    Two-player career

    The duel over 3, 5 or 8 seasons. Both teams age, lose staff and redraft every offseason, with shared draws and the first pick switching each time.

    Team database

    Browse every team-season and driver in the game with all their ratings. Search, filter and sort to plan your draft.

    Hall of fame

    Your best seasons, careers and duels, plus records like most wins and points. Saved in this browser after every result.
